## Stories in the image

1. **NVIDIA — Huang is back**
   Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president and CEO on
   Monday 23 November, eleven days after he resigned (12 Nov) following
   revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with a businesswoman US
   officials suspect of spying for China. The board said it saw no national
   security risk after input from senior US government officials; President
   Trump welcomed the decision. Nvidia shares jumped about 33% on Monday,
   the Nasdaq rose 6% and the S&P 500 4%.

2. **UKRAINE — War reaches Libya**
   Before dawn on Sunday 22 November, Ukrainian forces launched seven
   explosive-laden sea drones at Russian vessels in Benghazi port, Libya.
   The corvette Merkury — one of Russia's newest warships, commissioned in
   2023 — was sunk, a Ka-27 helicopter aboard her was destroyed, and the
   cargo ship MV Angara, which Kyiv says Russia used to transport weapons,
   was hit. President Zelenskyy claimed the operation on Monday 23 November;
   Libya's Tripoli government condemned it and said it had not authorised
   any foreign military activity in its waters.
   Sources: Wall Street Journal, 24 Nov 2026
   (https://www.wsj.com/world/inside-the-ukrainian-attack-that-sank-russian-warship-docked-in-libya-2dbd658b);
   Associated Press, 23 Nov 2026

3. **RUSSIA — War dead past 700,000**
   A joint Meduza/Mediazona investigation published Monday 23 November,
   based on leaked Russian state statistics (Rosstat) death records obtained
   by the hacker groups Belarusian Cyber Partisans and Silent Crow, found at
   least 490,000 registered Russian military deaths through September 2026.
   Accounting for missing soldiers and registration lags, the estimate puts
   total Russian war dead above 700,000 — with total casualties (killed and
   wounded) on track to exceed 2 million, Russia's deadliest conflict since
   World War II.
   Sources: New York Post, 23 Nov 2026
   (https://nypost.com/2026/11/23/world-news/russia-has-lost-astonishing-700k-soldiers-since-start-of-ukraine-war-1-in-every-200-citizens-killed-study-finds/);
   Bytes Europe, 23 Nov 2026 (https://www.byteseu.com/2542054/).

4. **CLIMATE — COP31 collapses**
   The COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye, ended Sunday 22 November
   with no overarching agreement after running more than 40 hours past its
   scheduled close — the longest UN climate conference on record and the
   first full collapse since Copenhagen in 2009. The EU, UK, Norway and
   small-island states rejected the draft "Antalya Pact" because it dropped
   fossil-fuel language and a $120 billion adaptation-finance target.
   Unresolved items carry over to COP32 in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, in
   November 2027.
   Sources: Drishti IAS, 23 Nov 2026
   (https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/31st-conference-of-the-parties-cop-31-to-the-unfccc);
   The Campus Review, 23 Nov 2026
   (https://thecampusreview.com/news/un-climate-talks-collapse/).

5. **MARKETS — AI rally roars back**
   Nvidia's 33% Monday surge ignited a global relief rally on Tuesday
   24 November: Asian shares rebounded after eight straight losing sessions,
   with Japan's Nikkei up nearly 5% as trading resumed after a long weekend,
   South Korea's Kospi up over 7%, and the MSCI Asia Pacific index up 1.8%.
   Wall Street had its best day since 9 April 2025.
   Sources: CNBC TV18, 24 Nov 2026
   (https://www.tradingview.com/news/cnbctv:69c712804094b:0/);
   Forex.com, 24 Nov 2026

6. **INDONESIA — Banten factory fire**
   A fire that broke out on Saturday evening 21 November at the PT Fonusa
   Agung Mulia confectionery factory (Milkita candies) in Cikande, Banten,
   killed at least 24 people, Indonesian authorities confirmed on Monday
   23 November, with dozens of workers still missing as rescuers searched
   the unstable three-storey building. About 300 workers were on shift;
   the suspected cause is an air-conditioner short circuit.
   Sources: BusinessWorld, 23 Nov 2026
   (https://bworldonline.com/world/2026/11/23/796078/indonesian-authorities-say-24-killed-in-factory-blaze-as-rescuers-search-for-dozens-missing/);
